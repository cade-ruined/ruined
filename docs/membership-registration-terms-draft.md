### 1. Who this is between and what these terms cover

These terms are between you and **The Ruined Project LLC**, operating as Ruined
("Ruined," "we," or "us"). They apply when you affirmatively accept them while
creating your Ruined registration account. They cover registration and the
account and community features we make available to you. Questions go to
connect@theruinedproject.com.

Registration does not purchase a paid membership. A later paid membership
requires a separate offer, paid membership agreement, and payment authorization.
These terms do not replace any separate paid agreement you have accepted.

### 2. Registration, eligibility, and profile access

Registration is currently available to adults age 18 and over in the United
States. Use your own account and provide accurate information. Keep access to
your email secure, do not share sign-in codes, and tell us if you suspect someone
else has accessed your account. Keep your contact and fulfillment details current.

To complete registration, verify your email, provide the required personal
details, and complete the card-saving step described below if it applies to you.
An approved complimentary registration does not require a payment card.

We will send a welcome email confirming your completed registration. Your member
profile remains locked until it is separately released, and we will send another
email when it is ready. A registration confirmation is not confirmation that your
profile, Foundations, Circle, or other member experiences are already available.

Ruined is a creative community built around learning, making, and showing up for
one another. Resources may be unfinished, unavailable, or changed as the experience
develops. Current listings explain what is available; screenshots, example data,
and roadmap ideas do not by themselves promise delivery. Registration does not
guarantee business results, employment, earnings, admission to a particular
Circle, or access to every event or product.

### 3. Saving a card — no charge or subscription

Where registration requires a card, Stripe collects and stores it for a future
checkout you choose to complete. Before saving it, you will be asked separately
to agree to that storage. Your Ruined account may display limited details such as
the card brand, last four digits, and expiry date so you can identify the saved
method.

**Saving a card does not charge you, start a paid membership or subscription,
create a purchase commitment, or authorize Ruined to charge you automatically.**
It does not reserve a membership rate, founding offer, membership place, or
Circle place. Neither accepting these terms nor receiving a welcome or
profile-ready email gives us permission to charge the card.

We may make the saved card available in a later checkout that you choose to
complete. You must separately confirm that checkout before a payment. Any
recurring payment requires its own disclosed terms and your separate affirmative
authorization; registration and card-storage permission do not provide it.

You can withdraw permission to keep an unused saved card and remove it through
the saved-card controls before starting a future checkout. If your registration
requires a card, you will need to save one again to complete registration. Your
personal details remain saved when you remove the card. Contact
connect@theruinedproject.com if you need help removing it or closing your
registration account.

Complimentary registration skips this card requirement. It does not automatically
convert to paid membership or authorize a future charge.

### 4. A later membership offer, Circles, and separate purchases

You may decline any later paid offer. Before you choose a paid membership, we
will present its actual price, applicable tax, billing frequency, included
benefits, any minimum commitment, renewal and cancellation terms, and the paid
agreement that applies. A rate or benefit shown elsewhere does not become yours
merely because you registered or saved a card.

A Circle is a small member group; its Shaper helps guide it. **Confirmed couples
who register together will be placed in the same Circle.** Each person must
register with their own account and confirm the pairing by entering the other's
email address. The pairing is confirmed only after both people confirm. Contact
Ruined if you need to change a confirmed pairing.

Being placed together does not guarantee a particular Circle. You may request a
particular Circle through support; that choice depends on suitability and
available places. Confirming a pairing does not start a paid couples plan or give
one person authority to accept paid terms or authorize payment for the other.

An actual Shopify purchase or separately ticketed event is separate from this
registration. Its price and applicable terms must be disclosed before you
choose to purchase.

### 5. How we treat one another

Be respectful, be honest, and respect other people's boundaries. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate unlawfully, impersonate someone, spam, solicit members
without permission, interfere with the service, or share content you have no
right to share. Do not record meetings or publish another member's image,
messages, or personal story without their permission.

Keep nonpublic Circle discussions and other members' private information within
the intended group. This expectation does not prevent reporting misconduct,
seeking professional advice, complying with law, or getting help in an
emergency. We cannot guarantee that every member will honor it, so share
thoughtfully and report concerns to support.

### 6. Your work, your information, and communications

Your original work remains yours. By submitting content to Ruined, you give us
permission to store, display, and process it only as needed to provide the
features and sharing you use, support your account, and meet legal obligations.
This does not give us permission to use your portrait, personal story, or work
in public advertising. We would ask separately for that permission.

Ruined's learning materials remain owned by Ruined or their respective owners.
You may use materials you can access for your own learning; do not resell,
redistribute, or publicly republish them without permission or another lawful
basis. Use your own exported timeline as you wish, while respecting anyone
else's privacy and intellectual property.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information and
how to make a privacy request. We process registration and account details,
consent records, saved-payment-method references, profile content, progress,
registrations, and support information to operate the features you use. Access
depends on the feature, your sharing choices, and authorized staff
responsibilities. The member app is not an end-to-end encrypted journal or a
confidential counseling service. Do not submit sensitive information that is
unnecessary for the feature.

Google Meet, Google Chat, and Calendar are separate services. Using them may
reveal your Google display name, email, or contributions to other participants,
and their own terms and privacy practices apply. We may send essential
verification, registration, profile-ready, account, security, support, and event
communications. Marketing choices remain separate; accepting these terms does
not subscribe you to marketing. Contact connect@theruinedproject.com for privacy
questions or requests.

### 7. Events, guidance, and safety

Participation is voluntary. Decide whether an activity is suitable for you and
follow the safety instructions provided. An event may require separate
registration, eligibility checks, terms, or an activity-specific waiver. These
registration terms are not a blanket release for physical activities.

Ruined's educational content and member conversations are not a substitute for
qualified medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or other professional care.
Neither the app nor support is an emergency service. Contact local emergency
services if immediate help is needed.

### 8. Leaving, changes, and account restrictions

You may withdraw from this registration at any time by contacting
connect@theruinedproject.com. There is no fee to leave this registration.
Request data access or deletion separately; leaving does not automatically
erase records we need for legal, security, or dispute purposes. If you later
choose a paid membership, its separately accepted cancellation terms apply to
that membership.

We may restrict or end access to address a material breach of these terms,
security risks, or threats to the community. Where reasonably possible, we will
explain the reason and provide a way to ask us to review the decision. Immediate
restrictions may be necessary for safety or security.

We may change or end this registration phase, with reasonable notice where
practical. We will not use a change to impose charges or take ownership of your
work. We will notify you of material changes to these terms and obtain renewed
agreement where required; changes will not rewrite the version you previously
accepted.

### 9. Resolving concerns and accepting these terms

Start with connect@theruinedproject.com so we can try to resolve concerns. This
does not limit your right to contact a regulator, obtain legal advice, or use
any remedy available under applicable law. Nothing here excludes rights or
liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. These terms do not require
arbitration, waive class actions, or impose a blanket liability cap.

By selecting the acceptance checkbox and submitting it, you confirm that you
have read and agree to these registration terms, are at least 18, and acknowledge
the Privacy Policy. Ruined will retain the accepted text, its version,
and the acceptance record. Accepting these terms does not itself save a card,
authorize a payment, or enroll you in paid membership.

## Implementation and source notes — do not publish with the agreement

- Keep this agreement separate from `ruined_membership-v1`, the existing
  test-pilot agreement that prohibits real cards, and from the unpublished paid
  agreement draft. Do not relabel or silently replace either accepted text.
- Keep account-terms acceptance, Privacy Policy acknowledgment, card-storage
  permission, marketing choices, and any future recurring-payment authorization
  distinguishable. Link the complete candidate and current Privacy Policy at the
  acceptance control. Do not precheck, infer, or backfill acceptance for someone.
- The account-creation notice is: "I agree to the Membership Terms and
  acknowledge the Privacy Policy. Registration and saving a card do not start a
  paid membership or authorize a charge." This short notice links to the full
  registration terms; it does not replace them or the separate card-storage
  choice.
- Publish only the exact owner-approved candidate body as a versioned, hashed
  agreement. Preserve the body and acceptance evidence for the version actually
  shown. A local demonstration of these terms is not a real acceptance or a
  published agreement. This file creates neither.
- Confirm the live Privacy Policy and its stable version/acknowledgment record
  before publication. Acknowledging a privacy notice is not blanket permission
  for unrelated data uses, public advertising, or marketing.
- The existing `save-payment-method-v1` storage permission is the separate
  card-step consent. Its approved purpose is a future checkout the person
  chooses to complete. `mode: "setup"` and a provider's technical ability to
  charge a saved method are not commercial consent. Do not turn storage consent
  into authorization for off-session or recurring payments.
- Keep completion dependent on the required details and saved method unless
  exempt. Preserve card removal and the registered/profile-release distinction.
  Do not issue paid membership or promise a rate or place because a method was
  saved. Review withdrawal handling and access/deletion requests against the
  retained pilot provisions, without promising a new automatic deletion system.
- Source provisions are `docs/membership-pilot-agreement-draft.md`. The current
  flow was checked against `src/lib/stripe/payment-method-model.ts`,
  `src/lib/stripe/payment-method-service.ts`,
  `src/components/membership/MemberPaymentMethod.tsx`,
  `src/components/membership/MemberRegistrationReceipt.tsx`, and
  `src/lib/membership/registration-couple.ts`. The paid-agreement draft was read
  for separation of scope, not incorporated as accepted registration policy.
- The same-Circle wording reflects the owner's explicit policy for couples who
  register together. Reciprocal verified-account confirmation is implemented in
  `db/migrations/20260930210000_registration_couples.sql`; its confirmed pairs
  use the same-Circle placement checks in
  `db/migrations/20260930113000_couple_circle_placement.sql`.
  `tests/registration-couples.test.mjs` covers rejecting separate Circle
  assignments before payment and moving a confirmed pair together. Availability
  of a particular Circle does not qualify the promise to place the pair together.
- Official Stripe guidance checked September 30, 2026: Checkout setup mode
  can save a method without an initial payment. Stripe directs merchants to
  explain and obtain permission for the specific intended use; charging while
  the customer is absent requires additional terms addressing transaction
  authorization, timing/frequency, amount determination, and applicable
  cancellation. This draft limits the current use to a later checkout the
  member chooses and separately confirms.
  [Stripe: save and reuse payment methods](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-and-reuse?payment-ui=embedded-components),
  [Stripe: Checkout setup mode](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout/save-and-reuse?payment-ui=checkout-form).
- For a later online negative-option offer, ROSCA addresses disclosure of
  material terms before billing information is obtained, informed consent before
  a charge, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. Saving a card now is not
  a substitute for that later offer and consent. This observation does not
  establish that all federal, state, or card-network requirements have been met.
  [15 U.S.C. § 8403, official U.S. Code](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=%28title%3A15+section%3A8403+edition%3Aprelim%29).
- This is drafting assistance for owner and qualified legal review, not a legal
