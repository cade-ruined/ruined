# Public member card

Members edit their profile normally at `/my/profile`. Their display name, selected profile photo, biography, building text, location, website and earned labels feed the same worn, two-sided card. The profile has three actions: **Edit profile · My Card · My Invitation**. My Card opens the archive room; it has sharing controls and a link back to the single profile editor, with no separate copy of editable card text.

The card keeps the existing Ruined wordmark, exact leaf and IvyOra/Inter fonts, deterministic wear, thin 3D body, lighting, drag, tap/keyboard flip, still view and front/back PNG downloads. Long names remain complete. Long secondary text becomes an excerpt on the physical card; the readable disclosure retains the full chosen profile text.

Public card sharing is separate from Circle sharing and starts off. Members select fields in Edit profile, then enable a stable random `/card/<token>` link. They can make it private again, including from a paused membership. New saves update the selected public fields automatically. Sharing a portrait makes later photo changes public immediately; that behavior is explained alongside the choice.

## Personal invitation

My Invitation opens the invitation card and a private creation/history dashboard in the same room. The member enters a recipient's name and email, then creates an independent invitation. The optional **Email this invitation** choice queues a personal email from Ruined; leaving it unchecked creates a link to copy. Every invitation gets its own random `/invitation/<token>` URL and a fixed 48-hour deadline starting at creation. Creating another does not replace earlier invitations. The recipient's name appears in the handwritten card line and prefills the request form; their email stays owner-only. The recipient must submit that matching email. Invitations work while My Card is private and disclose only the inviter's public name/tag, recipient name and deadline. They preserve the existing waitlist, admission, email verification and membership entry process. The public action remains **Request to join**.

A valid invitation is attached to a fresh waitlist request, then bound to the same admitted person using their verified email. Completion is recorded only when entry, agreement/profile setup and active paid or complimentary membership satisfy the established checks. Unique attribution and immutable completion timestamps prevent repeat submissions, renewals and retries from increasing the count. Self referrals and existing completed members are excluded. Duplicate-email requests retain their original attribution and cannot gain or replace one.

The owner sees recipient names/emails, created and expiry dates, independent email status, request/completion status, and counts of created, active, expired and joined invitations. **Requested** records a matching submission; **Joined** requires completed membership and includes credit from earlier shared invitations. Ruined administrators retain the existing joined-member records. Cancelling a personal invitation prevents new submissions and hides its public page while retaining its history; a legitimate earlier request can still complete and receive its original credit. Existing shared invitation URLs retain their original deadline and compatibility. An invalid, expired, revoked or mismatched-email token is rejected before any interest or outbox row is written. Ordinary waitlist submissions without an invitation remain available.

Migration `20260923000000_personal_member_invitations.sql` adds private recipient records, delivery state, immutable creation/expiry fields and nullable referral links. Creating is limited to 20 per rolling 24 hours per member; active duplicates for the same recipient and self-invitations are rejected. Creation request IDs make retries safe. The stored email payload and provider idempotency key remain identical across retries, capped at five attempts within 23 hours of the first attempt. Immediate sends run after the creation response; the existing communications worker provides a retry backstop. **Email sent** means provider acceptance, not verified delivery or opening. Creating an invitation does not subscribe the recipient to marketing. Member deletion purges recipient and delivery data while preserving existing historical referral credit.

## Physical card refinement

The public view centers the card inside a photographic basement archive: worn concrete, dark drawers, a scuffed worktable and warm light. Dedicated landscape and portrait backgrounds preserve the room on phones and desktop. This room belongs only to the card view; the existing walk is unchanged. The stock is an actual closed mesh with a shallow bow, twist, handled corner, 0.8 mm thickness, layered cut edge and small paper fibers. Separate face-aligned roughness and bump maps combine photographed paper detail with stable oxidation, coating abrasion and corner wear. Both faces use the website’s Ink palette and shipped printers-ink texture (#141413, with bone text), including the downloadable artwork and social preview. Matching face materials keep the texture visible as either face turns. The portrait field is reserved for the chosen profile picture and stays blank when no picture is shared. The exact supplied leaf appears once on each footer as a masked metallic and iridescent foil stamp, enlarged to 224 px in the 1408 px artwork with 78 px of bottom clearance; a more polished surface and stronger iridescence make its reflected color change more visibly with viewing angle. Downloads and link images carry a static foil impression. The background uses a denser field of larger, irregular dust flecks, softly defocused foreground motes and occasional faint exact-leaf hints. Archive dust drifts visibly at roughly 3 pixels per second rather than the earlier subpixel pace; the editor retains its quieter motion. About 540 particles fill a 1440 by 900 room and 194 fill a 390 by 844 phone, with caps of 650 and 230 respectively. Room fill keeps particles visible outside the beam; they brighten further in the shaft from the photographed pendant toward its pool on the table. The edge is slightly thicker for readable depth. Archive lighting follows the visible pendant and subtle table bounce, with a soft viewer-side fill (intensity 1.7) lifting the ink slightly while preserving the pendant highlights and shadow; the editor keeps its studio lighting. A softly feathered shadow is projected from the same archive source onto a safe table plane and composited only across the photographed worktop. Reflections follow the warped surface; drag rotates far enough to inspect either face and settles to the nearest resting face. Dust and quiet idle movement pause offscreen, in background tabs and for reduced motion.

Fixed the first version's 3D startup defect: React Three Fiber mounts native canvas fallback children even with working WebGL, so an effect in the old fallback disabled the scene. The fallback is now inert, readiness follows the first actual mesh draw, and actual renderer failures use a visible-time startup timeout plus the static fallback. Reduced-motion remounts, drag cancellation and long readable names were also corrected. Camera framing now fits transformed stock and fiber bounds to the actual canvas dimensions on every rendered frame, including the lifted card and the middle of a flip; it immediately moves back when clearance is needed and eases closer only when safe. The static fallback also scales to narrow containers without changing the card’s proportions. The 32 focused geometry, camera-framing, archive-lighting, foil-material, layout, public-page and share-image checks pass; geometry checks include welded closure, finite normals, readable reverse UVs, consistent stock thickness and seeded wear. Browser/GPU interaction remains unverified because the browser security service denied access.

## Data and consent

`member_public_cards` stores publication and selected-field choices, selected milestone IDs, version and the random capability token. Text is projected from the current normal profile; old copied columns are retained only for migration rollback compatibility and are no longer read or written by the application. The new profile website field preserves an existing saved card website on migration. Joined dates come from `membership_activated_at`. Founding status and member numbers are not invented. Journal, Timeline, contacts, billing, private notes and legal/profile evidence never enter the public projection.

Profile text and field choices save in one transaction. Editable-profile revision and card-version checks reject a stale editor and roll back the complete operation. A member can hide a field and replace its content with private text in the same save without publishing the new text. Public reads project the latest selected sources and recheck the source revision before returning. Updated chosen milestone titles follow their source; unavailable labels disappear. Portrait bytes use a separate consent-gated endpoint and recheck the active role, selected field, version and photo path after loading. Private photo routes remain unchanged.

`member_invitations` stores invitation consent, token, version, issued_at and expires_at. Each issued link has exactly 48 hours from server creation. Copying, sharing, viewing and profile edits never extend it. Explicit renewal, re-enabling and replacing an expired invitation issue a new token; older tokens stay unavailable. Existing invitations retain their original creation date when the expiry migration is applied. Earlier accepted referrals can still complete after expiry. `member_referrals` holds immutable attribution and completion, with private database functions binding only verified admitted people. Both tables enable RLS and deny direct public/client access. Only a member's own API can disclose their count; the joined-record endpoint requires an operations administrator.

Public pages and media responses are unindexed, no-store and suppress referrers. Next's image optimizer accepts static assets only, so it cannot cache revocable card media. Card and invitation pages suppress outbound WebVitals reporting because performance entries can contain capability URLs. Downloads and external social caches cannot be recalled.

## Routes

- `/my/profile`: normal profile editor, including website and public card choices.
- `/my/card`: authenticated owner card room; edit, publish/withdraw, copy/share and download.
- `/my/invitation`: authenticated owner invitation room; create/renew/withdraw, copy/share, fixed expiry and private completed-join count.
- `/api/my/profile`: authenticated atomic profile and optional card-choice save.
- `/api/my/card`: authenticated GET/POST with origin, bounded body and version checks.
- `/api/my/invitation`: authenticated GET/PATCH with origin, bounded body and version checks.
- `/card/[token]`: public card; unknown, private or ineligible cards return unavailable.
- `/invitation/[token]`: public name-only invitation and existing request-to-join form.
- `/api/invitations/[token]`: limited public invitation projection.
- `/api/cards/[token]/portrait`: guarded owned image bytes.
- `/api/cards/[token]/image`: generated 1200×630 social preview, rechecking consent after rendering.
- `/api/ops/members/[memberId]/referrals`: administrator-only completed count and the latest 50 joined members.
- `/card/preview` and `/invitation/preview`: local development examples; disabled in production. Owner room previews are also read-only.

## Standalone archive room

Used by the public card, public invitation and both owner room views, plus their local previews. The room is a responsive photographic background; the card remains the existing interactive 3D object. The walk and its room transitions remain unchanged. The normal profile is the shared source of card content. Its decorative layers never receive pointer input, and the page still scrolls when details or a narrow viewport need more height. Reduced motion skips the room reveal and pauses the particles.

Two compressed WebP plates ship at roughly 120 KB each, selected by the viewport shape. Asset provenance and generation prompts are in `docs/member-card-archive-assets.md`. The larger foil reserves its own lower-right area on both faces, with metadata and website rows reflowed to clear it. Downloaded artwork and static share images include the enlarged stamp. The foil is a visual brand seal; current membership eligibility remains enforced by the existing public-card checks.

## Light registration

The archive image remains a photograph. Its lamp does not emit live light; a matching simulated source lights the 3D card. A shared crop model maps the lamp and tabletop from the landscape/portrait plates through the same centered cover scaling as the page image. Both the renderer and dust use those anchors. The exact original mark, portrait consent and card geometry are preserved. The larger foil has its own lower-right area, with text and rules clearing its bounds by at least 24 artwork pixels. The viewer-side fill does not cast an additional shadow.

The shadow is a soft approximation projected from the transformed card bounds through the simulated pendant onto a horizontal plane below the card’s complete movement envelope. Its resting footprint is calibrated to the photograph’s foreshortened table, preserving subsequent movement and shape changes. A decorative page-level SVG receives the projected polygon and clips it to the visible table, so the shadow can extend beyond the card canvas without covering the room wall. Still view, reduced-motion fallback and renderer teardown clear the live shadow. Scene measurements refresh on viewport/element changes and scrolling, including layout changes from card details and share status. Source selection follows the picture’s actual media query, including scrollbar cases. The lighting frame avoids repeated layout reads, and the reflection environment is generated once per renderer/mode rather than on drag frames. Standalone dust checks cover missing logo assets, particle caps, the 30 fps ceiling, reduced motion, visibility pausing and cleanup. Browser/GPU verification is still blocked by the browser security service.


## Invitation expiry

Migration `20260922200000_member_invitation_expiry.sql` must be applied before releasing the expiry code. The public page, API and waitlist attribution enforce the same server deadline. Open pages disable use at the deadline and recheck when a tab resumes. Cards and saved artwork display the stored expiry in America/Denver with MST/MDT; draft cards describe the 48-hour validity until a link is created. Local preview timestamps are example-only. No scheduled job is needed for expiration.

## Invitation link preview

Active invitation pages expose a versioned, brand-only spinning card MP4 through Open Graph video metadata and a JPEG poster for clients that display still previews. Apple Messages supports downloadable MP4 previews; animation remains controlled by the receiving app and its settings. A GIF of the same rotation is also shipped for direct use. Assets use the membership origin explicitly, since the root site's metadata base points to the separate public website.

The current public inviter name remains in the preview title. Static media contains no member names, tags, token, or expiry date, and does not confer access. Invalid, expired, or withdrawn invitations expose no preview media or identity. Token pages retain private/no-store and noindex headers; only versioned generic media is publicly cacheable. Messaging clients may retain previously generated previews independently of the website.

The offline generator is `scripts/render-invitation-share-preview.mjs`; it reuses the card artwork and exact supplied marks. Verify the poster, both faces in the rotation, MP4 playback, total resource size, and raw metadata in the initial HTML for preview crawlers before publishing.
