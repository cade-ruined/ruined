# Ruined Membership — Pilot Agreement

**Draft for Cade's approval and legal review. Not published or in effect.**

This is a proposed agreement for the invited, no-charge test pilot only. It is
not the commercial agreement for a future paid membership. Approve the exact
text before publishing a version in
the member app. The review notes after the agreement are not member terms.

## Proposed member-facing text

### 1. Who this is between

This agreement is between you and **The Ruined Project LLC**,
operating as Ruined ("Ruined," "we," or "us"). It applies when you affirmatively
accept it to join our invited membership pilot. Questions go to
connect@theruinedproject.com.

### 2. What you're joining

Ruined is a creative community built around learning, making, and showing up
for one another. This pilot lets invited members try the member profile,
Foundations, Circles, Academy resources, events, and support as we prepare
the experience for wider use.

This is an early release. Some resources may be unfinished, unavailable, or
changed during the pilot. The app's current listings explain what is available;
screenshots, example data, and roadmap ideas are not promises of delivery.
Membership does not guarantee business results, employment, earnings, a
particular Circle placement, or access to every event or product.

### 3. No real charges during this pilot

The pilot has no membership fee, purchase commitment, cancellation fee, or
automatic paid renewal. Any checkout specifically labeled as a test uses
Stripe test mode. Displayed test prices, subscriptions, and receipts simulate
billing; they do not create a debt or authorize us to charge you real money.
Use only the test payment details Ruined supplies, not a real payment card.

We will not convert your pilot account to paid membership automatically.
Any later paid membership requires a separate offer showing its actual price,
billing frequency, included benefits, renewal and cancellation terms, and your
separate affirmative consent before a real charge. You can decline that offer.

An actual Shopify purchase or separately ticketed event is outside this
no-charge pilot. Its price and applicable terms must be disclosed before you
choose to purchase. A test Artifact award does not promise a physical item or
shipment, including The First Coin, unless Ruined separately confirms that
fulfillment in writing.

### 4. Your account and your place

You must be at least 18 and use your own account with accurate information.
Keep access to your email secure, do not share sign-in codes, and tell us if you
suspect someone else has accessed your account. Keep contact and shipping
details current when they are needed for something you choose to receive.

A Circle is a small member group; its Shaper helps guide it. You may request a
specific Circle through support, but placement depends on suitability and
available places. You can begin Foundations before joining a Circle. Completing
Foundations requires both its required work and an active Circle assignment.
Shaper and operator responsibilities are separate access permissions, not
automatic promotions earned by completing lessons.

### 5. How we treat one another

Be respectful, be honest, and respect other people's boundaries. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate unlawfully, impersonate someone, spam, solicit members
without permission, interfere with the service, or share content you have no
right to share. Do not record meetings or publish another member's image,
messages, or personal story without their permission.

Keep nonpublic Circle discussions and other members' private information within
the intended group. This expectation does not prevent reporting misconduct,
seeking professional advice, complying with law, or getting help in an
emergency. We cannot guarantee that every member will honor it, so share
thoughtfully and report concerns to support.

### 6. Your work, your information, and communications

Your original work remains yours. By submitting content to Ruined, you give us
permission to store, display, and process it only as needed to provide the
features and sharing you use, support your account, and meet legal obligations.
This does not give us permission to use your portrait, personal story, or work
in public advertising. We would ask separately for that permission.

Ruined's learning materials remain owned by Ruined or their respective owners.
You may use materials you can access for your own learning; do not resell,
redistribute, or publicly republish them without permission or another lawful
basis. Use your own exported timeline as you wish, while respecting anyone
else's privacy and intellectual property.

We process account details, profile content, progress, registrations, and
support information to operate membership. Access depends on the feature,
your sharing choices, and authorized staff responsibilities. The member app
is not an end-to-end encrypted journal or a confidential counseling service.
Do not submit sensitive information that is unnecessary for the feature.

Google Meet, Google Chat, and Calendar are separate services. Using them may
reveal your Google display name, email, or contributions to other participants,
and their own terms and privacy practices apply. We may send essential account,
security, support, and event communications; marketing choices remain separate.
Contact connect@theruinedproject.com for privacy questions or requests.

### 7. Events, guidance, and safety

Participation is voluntary. Decide whether an activity is suitable for you and
follow the safety instructions provided. An event may require separate
registration, eligibility checks, terms, or an activity-specific waiver. This
membership agreement is not a blanket release for physical activities.

Ruined's educational content and member conversations are not a substitute for
qualified medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or other professional care.
Neither the app nor support is an emergency service. Contact local emergency
services if immediate help is needed.

### 8. Leaving, changes, and account restrictions

You may leave the pilot at any time by contacting connect@theruinedproject.com
or opening a membership support ticket. There is no fee to leave. Request data
access or deletion separately; leaving does not automatically erase records
we need for legal, security, or dispute purposes.

We may restrict or end access to address a material breach of these terms,
security risks, or threats to the community. Where reasonably possible, we will
explain the reason and provide a way to ask us to review the decision. Immediate
restrictions may be necessary for safety or security.

We may change or end the pilot, with reasonable notice where practical. We will
not use a change to impose real charges or take ownership of your work. We will
notify you of material changes to these terms and obtain renewed agreement
where required; changes will not rewrite the version you previously accepted.

### 9. Resolving concerns and accepting these terms

Start with connect@theruinedproject.com so we can try to resolve concerns. This
does not limit your right to contact a regulator, obtain legal advice, or use
any remedy available under applicable law. Nothing here excludes rights or
liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. This pilot agreement does not
require arbitration, waive class actions, or impose a blanket liability cap.

By selecting the acceptance checkbox and submitting it, you confirm that you
have read and agree to this pilot agreement and that you are at least 18. Ruined
will retain the accepted text, its version, and the acceptance record. Accepting
this agreement does not itself authorize a real payment.

  automated deletion that the product does not support.

  entity and location, offer/inclusions, price/tax, commitment length, recurring
  consent, reminders, cancellation/access-end behavior, refunds, event and
  physical-product terms, and laws applicable to members' locations. Verify
  these operationally rather than relying on contractual wording alone.
- **Legal review:** this is drafting assistance, not legal advice or a finding
  of compliance. Have a qualified attorney review the agreement and actual
  offer, especially before taking real payments or running physical activities.

### Sources checked September 4, 2026

The drafting approach separates test access from a later paid offer. For online
recurring charges, ROSCA requires clear material terms, informed consent, and a
simple way to stop recurring charges. See [15 U.S.C. section 8403 in the enacted
ROSCA text](https://www.congress.gov/111/plaws/publ345/PLAW-111publ345.pdf).

Utah's current Automatic Renewal Contracts Act has its own defined coverage,
renewal notices, and trial-offer notices. Its automatic-renewal definition
uses a subsequent paid term longer than 45 days; do not assume all membership
plans have identical notice requirements. See [Utah Code, Title 13, Chapter 70,
sections 101 and 201 (PDF pages 418–419)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/C13_1800010118000101.pdf).
Other applicable jurisdictions still require review.
