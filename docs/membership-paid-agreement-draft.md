## Proposed member-facing agreement

### 1. You and Ruined

This agreement is between you and **The Ruined Project LLC**, operating as Ruined
("Ruined," "we," or "us"). Contact connect@theruinedproject.com with questions.
You must be at least **18**, use your own account, and provide accurate information.
Keep your email secure, do not share sign-in codes, and report suspected unauthorized
access. Keep contact and shipping details current when needed for something you
choose to receive.

### 2. Your membership

Ruined is a creative community built around learning, making, and showing up for one
another. Membership includes the guided Foundations experience, a small Circle led
by a Shaper, Academy lessons and resources, and your personal journal and member
space. It also provides opportunities to connect through the gatherings listed by
Ruined. Both payment plans provide the same membership.

Circle placement depends on suitability and available places. You can begin
Foundations while your Circle is being arranged; completing Foundations requires
its required work and an active Circle assignment. Shaper and operator roles are
separate permissions, not promotions automatically earned by completing lessons.

Each event lists its own availability, access conditions and any separate cost.
Membership does not include every event, garment, artifact or other physical item
at no additional charge. Store purchases and separately ticketed experiences have
their own disclosed terms. Example imagery and roadmap ideas are not delivery
promises. Membership does not guarantee business results, employment, earnings,
or a particular Circle placement.

### 3. Price, payment and automatic renewal

**You choose one recurring plan:**

| Plan | First payment | Automatic renewal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | **$499 USD at signup** for the first month | **$499 USD each month** until canceled |
| Annual | **$5,040 USD at signup** for the full year | **$5,040 USD each year** until canceled |

The annual plan is paid in full upfront. Its $420 monthly equivalent is a comparison,
not a monthly installment option. There is no free trial for paid membership.
Checkout shows the applicable tax treatment and final amount before you authorize
payment. Your membership begins when payment is confirmed. Your billing page shows
your subscription period and next renewal date.

Payment is processed by Stripe. After accepting this agreement, you must separately
authorize the selected recurring payment before checkout. That authorization allows
Ruined, through Stripe, to charge the selected initial amount and its recurring
renewals until you cancel. Accepting the agreement alone does not authorize payment.
We retain the plan, amount, frequency and your affirmative acceptance record.

**To stop the next renewal, cancel before its renewal date using the billing controls
in your member account.** You can also contact connect@theruinedproject.com or open
a membership support ticket. There is no cancellation fee. Cancellation stops
future renewals; access continues through the term already paid for, subject to the
account restrictions in section 7. Cancellation does not automatically refund the
current term. Refund requests are reviewed individually; this does not limit any
refund or cancellation right provided by applicable law.

We provide renewal notices required by applicable law. A price change will not
change a term you have already paid for. We will disclose a proposed new price
before it applies and obtain your authorization before charging that new price.
Stripe may retry a failed authorized payment; paid features may be restricted until
billing is resolved. We do not promise a particular grace period.

### 4. Complimentary invitations and pilot access

A complimentary invitation states that membership is complimentary and any applicable
end date. It skips payment. Only an authorized Ruined operator can grant
complimentary membership. A standard invitation follows the paid signup process.

**Complimentary or no-charge pilot access never automatically becomes a paid
subscription.** A later paid membership requires a disclosed offer, acceptance of
the applicable paid terms, and your separate payment authorization. You may decline.
A displayed Stripe test payment does not authorize a real charge.

### 5. Conduct, privacy and your work

Be respectful and honest. Do not harass, threaten, unlawfully discriminate,
impersonate others, spam, solicit members without permission, interfere with the
service, or share material you have no right to share. Do not record meetings or
publish another member's image, messages or personal story without permission.

Keep nonpublic Circle discussions and other members' private information within
the intended group. This does not prevent reporting misconduct, seeking professional
advice, complying with law, or getting emergency help. We cannot guarantee every
member will honor that expectation; share thoughtfully and report concerns.

Your original work remains yours. You permit Ruined to store, display and process
content you submit only as needed for the features and sharing you use, account
support and legal obligations. This does not permit public advertising use of your
portrait, story or work; we would ask separately. Ruined's learning materials remain
owned by Ruined or their respective owners. Use accessible materials for your own
learning; do not resell, redistribute or publicly republish them without permission
or another lawful basis. You may use your exported timeline while respecting
others' privacy and intellectual property.

We process account details, profile content, progress, registrations and support
information to operate membership. Access depends on the feature, your sharing
choices and authorized staff responsibilities. The app is not an end-to-end
encrypted journal or confidential counseling service. Do not submit unnecessary
sensitive information.

Google Meet, Chat and Calendar are separate services with their own terms and
privacy practices. Participation may reveal your Google display name, email or
contributions to other participants. We may send essential account, billing,
security, support and event communications; marketing choices remain separate.
Send privacy or data requests to connect@theruinedproject.com. Canceling a
subscription does not itself delete account records.

### 6. Events, guidance and safety

Participation is voluntary. Decide whether an activity is suitable for you and
follow its safety instructions. An event may require separate registration,
eligibility checks, terms or an activity-specific waiver. This agreement is not a
blanket release for physical activities.

Educational content and member conversations do not replace qualified medical,
mental-health, legal, financial or other professional care. The app and support
are not emergency services; contact local emergency services for immediate help.

### 7. Account restrictions and changes

We may restrict or end access to address a material breach, security risk or threat
to the community. Where reasonably possible, we will explain the reason and provide
a way to request review. Immediate action may be necessary for safety or security.
Contact us about any related billing or refund request; applicable rights remain
in place.

We will notify you of material membership or agreement changes and seek renewed
agreement where required. Changes do not rewrite terms you previously accepted or
transfer ownership of your work. Requests to delete an account or its data are
separate from canceling renewal; we may retain records needed for legal, security
or dispute purposes.

### 8. Concerns and acceptance

Contact connect@theruinedproject.com so we can try to resolve a concern. This does
not limit your ability to seek legal advice, contact a regulator or use remedies
available under applicable law. Nothing here excludes rights or liability that
cannot lawfully be excluded. This agreement does not require arbitration, waive
class actions or impose a blanket liability cap.

By selecting the agreement checkbox and submitting it, you confirm that you have
read and agree to these terms and are at least 18. Ruined retains the accepted
text, version and acceptance record. Paid signup also requires the separate
recurring-payment authorization described in section 3.

## Implementation and source notes — do not publish with the agreement

- Source provisions: `docs/membership-pilot-agreement-draft.md`. Offer checked against
  `src/data/public-membership.ts` and `src/components/public-members/MembershipOverview.tsx`;
  amounts checked against `src/lib/membership/pricing.ts`.
- Display the selected amount, upfront timing, renewal frequency, cancellation route,
  tax treatment and final total prominently before collecting payment details.
  A link to this agreement alone is not the billing disclosure. Keep the recurring
  checkbox unchecked and separate from agreement acceptance.
- Verify that the member billing controls really stop renewals, preserve the agreed
  access end date, and show a confirmation. Match the final refund/cancellation
  choice across the portal configuration, app access rules and agreement.
- The FTC's current ROSCA guidance describes pre-payment disclosure of material
  terms, informed consent and a simple way to stop recurring charges. This supports
  the disclosure structure here; it is not a complete jurisdictional assessment.
  [FTC ROSCA recap](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2018/07/time-rosca-recap-ftc-says-risk-free-trial-was-risky-not-free).
- Utah's published Automatic Renewal Contracts Act defines covered renewal terms
  by reference to a subsequent paid term longer than 45 days and specifies a notice
  30–60 days before covered renewals, including the renewal date, total cost and
  cancellation options. Confirm applicability and other member-location rules;
  do not assume monthly and annual plans have identical notice requirements.
  [Utah Code, Title 13, Chapter 70](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter70/C13-70_2025010120240501.pdf).
- The public privacy notice and actual member-data practices need to remain aligned
  with the retained privacy provisions. This draft does not add a secrecy promise
  or claim automatic deletion of records the product retains.
