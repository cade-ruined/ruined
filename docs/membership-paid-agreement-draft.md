## Proposed member-facing agreement

### 1. You and Ruined

This agreement is between you and **The Ruined Project LLC**, operating as Ruined
("Ruined," "we," or "us"). Contact connect@theruinedproject.com with questions.
Membership is offered to members in the **United States**. You must be at least
**18**, use your own account, and provide accurate information.
Keep your email secure, do not share sign-in codes, and report suspected unauthorized
access. Keep contact and shipping details current when needed for something you
choose to receive.

### 2. Your membership

Ruined is a creative community built around learning, making, and showing up for one
another. Membership includes the guided Foundations experience, a small Circle led
by a Shaper, Academy lessons and resources, and your personal journal and member
space. It also provides opportunities to connect through the gatherings listed by
Ruined. Monthly and annual payment options provide the same membership benefits.
An individual membership covers one adult. A couples membership covers two named
adults, each using a separate account.

Circle placement depends on suitability and available places. You can begin
Foundations while your Circle is being arranged; completing Foundations requires
its required work and an active Circle assignment. Shaper and operator roles are
separate permissions, not promotions automatically earned by completing lessons.

Each event lists its own availability, access conditions and any separate cost.
Membership does not include every event, garment, artifact or other physical item
at no additional charge. Store purchases and separately ticketed experiences have
their own disclosed terms. Example imagery and roadmap ideas are not delivery
promises. Membership does not guarantee business results, employment, earnings,
or a particular Circle placement.

### 3. Price, payment and automatic renewal

**Paid membership begins with a 12-month commitment. Choose a payment option:**

| Membership and payment option | Payment at signup and billing amount, before tax | Initial 12-month price, before tax | Renewal after the initial commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Individual, paid monthly | **$499 USD at signup, then $499 each month** | **12 payments totaling $5,988 USD** | Monthly at $499 until canceled |
| Individual, paid annually | **$4,990 USD at signup** | **$4,990 USD paid upfront** | Annually at $4,990 until canceled |
| Founding individual, paid monthly, when eligible | **$349 USD at signup, then $349 each month** | **12 payments totaling $4,188 USD** | Monthly at $349 until canceled |
| Founding individual, paid annually, when eligible | **$3,490 USD at signup** | **$3,490 USD paid upfront** | Annually at $3,490 until canceled |
| Couples, paid monthly | **$699 USD at signup, then $699 each month** | **12 payments totaling $8,388 USD** | Monthly at $699 until canceled |
| Couples, paid annually | **$6,990 USD at signup** | **$6,990 USD paid upfront** | Annually at $6,990 until canceled |

**Founding membership.** Eligibility is checked when you join or rejoin. You qualify
if fewer than **50 other registered members are active** at that time, counting
both paid and complimentary members. Places become available again when the active
registered membership falls below 50. A complimentary member counts without being
required to start a paid subscription. An invitation or waitlist entry alone is not
an active registered membership. Each registered person in a couples membership
counts separately; the founding prices above apply to individual plans.
An offer may temporarily hold your quoted price and an available founding place
while you complete checkout; the offer shows when that hold expires.

The founding individual prices shown above remain available to an eligible member
for as long as that membership remains continuously active. Growth beyond 50
members does not remove benefits already held by a continuously active founding
member. Canceling ends founding benefits when the membership itself ends; merely
scheduling cancellation does not end benefits before that date.

If you cancel and later rejoin, your previous founding benefits do not automatically
return. You receive founding benefits only if you qualify under the current active
count when you rejoin; otherwise, the current standard terms apply. Your historical
member number does not determine eligibility. Founding eligibility does not
convert complimentary membership into paid membership without separate consent.

The monthly option is an initial annual commitment paid in 12 monthly installments;
it is not a month-to-month commitment during that first year. The annual option is
paid in full upfront. There is no free trial for paid membership. **Applicable tax
is added to the listed prices.** Checkout shows the tax and final amount before you
authorize payment. Your membership begins when payment is confirmed. Your billing
page shows your commitment end date, billing period and next payment date.

Payment is processed by Stripe. After accepting this agreement, you must separately
authorize the selected recurring payment before checkout. That authorization allows
Ruined, through Stripe, to charge the selected initial payment, any remaining
authorized installments in your initial commitment, and subsequent renewals until
canceled under these terms, together with applicable tax. Accepting the agreement
alone does not authorize payment. We retain the selected offer, amount, frequency,
commitment dates and your affirmative acceptance record.

**Use the billing controls in your member account to turn off automatic renewal
before its renewal date.** You can also contact connect@theruinedproject.com or open
a membership support ticket. For a monthly payment option during the initial
12-month commitment, turning off renewal prevents continuation after that
commitment; it does not itself release the remaining installments. After that
initial commitment, you can cancel the next monthly renewal without a cancellation
fee and retain access through the month already paid for, subject to section 7.

For a prepaid annual option, turning off the next annual renewal does not end the
year already paid for or trigger an early termination fee. Access continues through
that paid year, subject to section 7. A request to end the initial commitment early
is different from turning off its automatic renewal.

**Early exit from the initial monthly-payment commitment.** The early-exit charge
replaces the unpaid installments remaining in your initial 12-month commitment;
Ruined will not collect both that replacement charge and the installments it
replaces. The charge is **the lower of $1,500 USD or the unpaid installments
remaining in your initial commitment**, before any tax required by law. If only
$499 remains, the charge is $499; if $2,000 remains, the charge is $1,500.
After early cancellation, access continues through the period you have already
paid for, subject to section 7. The buyout does not purchase access for the rest
of the original year. The cancellation flow discloses the exact charge and
membership end date before you confirm early cancellation and authorize payment.
Turning off renewal alone does not trigger this charge. It does not apply to
complimentary membership or add a charge for turning off renewal of a prepaid year.
Refund and cancellation rights under applicable law remain in place.

Cancellation does not automatically refund payments already made. Refund requests
are reviewed individually; this does not limit any refund or cancellation right
provided by applicable law.

We provide renewal notices required by applicable law and payment-network rules.
A price change will not change the price of your initial commitment or a term you
have already paid for. The founding prices remain protected for an eligible,
continuously active member. We will disclose a proposed new price before it applies
and obtain your authorization before charging that new price.
Stripe may retry a failed authorized payment; paid features may be restricted until
billing is resolved. We do not promise a particular grace period.

### 4. Complimentary invitations and pilot access

A complimentary invitation states that membership is complimentary and any applicable
end date. It skips payment. Only an authorized Ruined operator can grant
complimentary membership. A standard invitation follows the paid signup process.

**Complimentary or no-charge pilot access never automatically becomes a paid
subscription.** A later paid membership requires a disclosed offer, acceptance of
the applicable paid terms, and your separate payment authorization. You may decline.
A displayed Stripe test payment does not authorize a real charge.

### 5. Conduct, privacy and your work

Be respectful and honest. Do not harass, threaten, unlawfully discriminate,
impersonate others, spam, solicit members without permission, interfere with the
service, or share material you have no right to share. Do not record meetings or
publish another member's image, messages or personal story without permission.

Keep nonpublic Circle discussions and other members' private information within
the intended group. This does not prevent reporting misconduct, seeking professional
advice, complying with law, or getting emergency help. We cannot guarantee every
member will honor that expectation; share thoughtfully and report concerns.

Your original work remains yours. You permit Ruined to store, display and process
content you submit only as needed for the features and sharing you use, account
support and legal obligations. This does not permit public advertising use of your
portrait, story or work; we would ask separately. Ruined's learning materials remain
owned by Ruined or their respective owners. Use accessible materials for your own
learning; do not resell, redistribute or publicly republish them without permission
or another lawful basis. You may use your exported timeline while respecting
others' privacy and intellectual property.

We process account details, profile content, progress, registrations and support
information to operate membership. Access depends on the feature, your sharing
choices and authorized staff responsibilities. The app is not an end-to-end
encrypted journal or confidential counseling service. Do not submit unnecessary
sensitive information.

Google Meet, Chat and Calendar are separate services with their own terms and
privacy practices. Participation may reveal your Google display name, email or
contributions to other participants. We may send essential account, billing,
security, support and event communications; marketing choices remain separate.
Send privacy or data requests to connect@theruinedproject.com. Canceling a
subscription does not itself delete account records.

### 6. Events, guidance and safety

Participation is voluntary. Decide whether an activity is suitable for you and
follow its safety instructions. An event may require separate registration,
eligibility checks, terms or an activity-specific waiver. This agreement is not a
blanket release for physical activities.

Educational content and member conversations do not replace qualified medical,
mental-health, legal, financial or other professional care. The app and support
are not emergency services; contact local emergency services for immediate help.

### 7. Account restrictions and changes

We may restrict or end access to address a material breach, security risk or threat
to the community. Where reasonably possible, we will explain the reason and provide
a way to request review. Immediate action may be necessary for safety or security.
Contact us about any related billing or refund request. We review refund requests
individually; applicable rights remain in place.

We will notify you of material membership or agreement changes and seek renewed
agreement where required. Changes do not rewrite terms you previously accepted or
transfer ownership of your work. Requests to delete an account or its data are
separate from canceling renewal; we may retain records needed for legal, security
or dispute purposes.

### 8. Concerns and acceptance

Contact connect@theruinedproject.com so we can try to resolve a concern. This does
not limit your ability to seek legal advice, contact a regulator or use remedies
available under applicable law. Nothing here excludes rights or liability that
cannot lawfully be excluded. This agreement does not require arbitration, waive
class actions or impose a blanket liability cap.

By selecting the agreement checkbox and submitting it, you confirm that you have
read and agree to these terms and are at least 18. Ruined retains the accepted
text, version and acceptance record. Paid signup also requires the separate
recurring-payment authorization described in section 3.

## Implementation and source notes — do not publish with the agreement

- Source provisions: `docs/membership-pilot-agreement-draft.md`, preserved without
  retroactive application. Revised prices, U.S. availability, added tax, minimum
  commitment and confirmed early-exit formula come from the owner's instructions;
  existing public copy and code are not evidence that this revised offer is implemented.
- Display the selected installment or upfront amount, full initial commitment,
  commitment end date, renewal frequency, final early termination rule, cancellation
  route, applicable tax and final total before collecting payment details.
  A link to this agreement alone is not the billing disclosure. Keep the recurring
  checkbox unchecked and separate from agreement acceptance.
- Verify that billing controls distinguish turning off renewal from ending the
  initial commitment early, preserve the agreed access end date, and show a
  confirmation. Match the final policy across consent, portal, access rules and
  agreement. Stripe's native period-end cancellation uses the current billing
  period, which is one month for a monthly price. Its documented flexible-billing
  prebilling can invoice remaining committed periods; it does not by itself define
  or authorize an arbitrary $1,500 fee. Do not enable the revised paid offer with
  the old cancellation flow.
  [Stripe portal](https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management);
  [Stripe prebilling](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/prebilling).
- ROSCA requires clear material disclosures before billing information is obtained,
  express informed consent and simple cancellation mechanisms. The 2026 Adobe
  proposed settlement and Shutterstock enforcement announcement specifically
  address obscured annual-paid-monthly commitments, fees and cancellation obstacles;
  they do not establish that all commitments or fees are prohibited.
  [15 U.S.C. §8403](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title15-section8403);
  [DOJ Adobe announcement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/adobe-agrees-150-million-settlement-and-injunction-resolve-alleged-violations-restore-online);
  [FTC Shutterstock announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/shutterstock-pay-35-million-settle-ftc-allegations-over-illegal-subscription-cancellation-practices).
- Fee enforceability requires the final clause and facts. Utah's *Commercial Real
  Estate v. Comcast*, 2012 UT 49, paragraphs 38–40, treats liquidated damages under
  ordinary contract defenses; Utah's consumer unconscionability rule also applies.
  California Civil Code §1671(c)–(d) has separate consumer restrictions. Neither
  a fixed amount nor the label "alternative termination" resolves those issues.
  [Official Utah opinion](https://legacy.utcourts.gov/committees/muji/materials/2012-09-10.pdf);
  [Utah §13-11-5](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter11/C13-11-S5_2025050720250507.pdf);
  [California §1671](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1671.).
- Utah's published Automatic Renewal Contracts Act defines covered renewal terms
  by reference to a subsequent paid term longer than 45 days and specifies a notice
  30–60 days before covered renewals, including the renewal date, total cost and
  cancellation options. Confirm applicability and other member-location rules;
  do not assume monthly and annual plans have identical notice requirements.
  [Utah Code, Title 13, Chapter 70](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter70/C13-70_2025010120240501.pdf).
- California requires a 15–45-day renewal notice for covered offers whose initial
  term is at least one year, an annual reminder, online cancellation of the renewal
  feature, and retained consent evidence for at least three years or one year after
  termination, whichever is longer. Section 17602(d)(2) distinguishes cancellation
  of renewal from other contract provisions, which remain subject to applicable
  law. Confirm the final first-anniversary treatment for monthly installments,
  including a transition from the initial annual commitment to month-to-month
  continuation. A worker that selects only Stripe prices with an annual billing
  interval does not cover that analysis or demonstrate the required reminders.
  [California §17602](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=17602.).
- Stripe's Mastercard guidance specifies an annual renewal reminder 7–30 days
  before billing. Mastercard's published rules also require subscription terms
  and cancellation instructions in that reminder, an upcoming-charge subject line,
  and separation from marketing. A 40-day notice alone does not cover this window.
  [Stripe recurring-billing guidance](https://support.stripe.com/questions/guidance-for-mastercard-recurring-billing-compliance-updates?locale=en-GB);
  [Mastercard Transaction Processing Rules, section 5.4.1(5), pages 187–188](https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/public/mastercardcom/na/global-site/documents/transaction-processing-rules.pdf).
- Operational recommendation, not an implemented or contractual promise: send
  annual notices at 40 and 20 days before renewal. Each should show the renewal
  date, actual total renewal cost including any applicable tax, recurring terms
  and a working cancellation URL.
  Keep evidence of delivery and test the actual email and destination. The two
  dates cover the Utah, California and Mastercard windows across the two sends,
  with room inside each window. Other applicable member-location requirements still
  need to be accounted for.
- The public privacy notice and actual member-data practices need to remain aligned
  with the retained privacy provisions. This draft does not add a secrecy promise
  or claim automatic deletion of records the product retains.
