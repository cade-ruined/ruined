# Foundations badge family and founder communications

Four transparent PNG patches use a red woven face, black stitched border, and large bone F1/F2/F3/F4 lettering. The Work uses the same assets as profile badge cards and downloads. The existing `your-story` award keeps its identity and now displays F1; `foundations-4` displays F4. F2/F3 remain visible locked course artwork with no invented completion requirements or awards.

F1/F4 assignment-release recap emails include the corresponding badge and an **Open The Work** link. Installing this update neither releases assignments nor queues recap messages. Existing frozen delivery payloads retain their bytes and idempotency.

Regular internal member-call reminders now recognize the existing canonical standing founder/team invitation. The same membership policy still governs access. Explicit cancelled or waitlisted registrations remain exclusions; public/external calls retain registration requirements. Founder-test calls require a registered ops invitation. Standing enrollment cannot authorize a reminder due before that invitation existed, while later team-role or Circle changes do not delay a valid ordinary invitation.

Email and SMS preserve consent, suppression, funding, current-authority checks, and deduplication. This adds no account grants, badge awards, migrations, manual sends, or release actions.

Validation before release: focused badge/recap UI suites; email and SMS repository/forecast regression suites; adjacent experience, founder invitation, operator funding and consent suites; TypeScript and ESLint; dark/light badge review at profile size and The Work course list.
